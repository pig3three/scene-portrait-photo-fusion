# Expanded pose library

Combine compatible components; do not cycle mechanically.

## Hands and arms

Relaxed asymmetric arms; opposing walking swing; one or both open palms; single/double V; finger heart; two-hand heart; tuck hair; shield soft light; adjust sleeve/cuff/collar; hold opposite wrist; hands joined low; hands behind back; use only visible pockets; hold own garment edge; lightly lift a flexible skirt edge when appropriate; present a real anchor; touch a safe surface; rest forearms on a real support; arms open during turn; one hand near collarbone; brief cheek frame; reach toward camera without covering face.

Do not invent pockets, props, rails, or surfaces. Keep fingers plausible and readable.

## Legs, feet, and weight

Parallel feet/soft knee; front-back stagger; light cross; wide grounded stance; side step; walking mid-stride; walking away with heel lifting; heel lifted/toe grounded; side toe tap; knee softly inward; knee bent backward with stable support; back foot lifted; half-turn pivot; full-turn transition; real safe low step; seated diagonal; seated crossed ankles; seated one foot forward; supported lean with both feet grounded; low crouch only when safe, comfortable, and wardrobe-appropriate.

## Torso and direction

Frontal; 20–30° turn; 45° three-quarter; clean side profile; back three-quarter/over-shoulder; gentle lean from ankles; small bend from hips with neutral spine; supported side lean; shoulder-leading walk; mid-turn; seated diagonal; upright stretch.

Avoid painful twists, deep back bends, or sexualized emphasis. Forward bends must be comfortable and age-appropriate.

## Gaze and motion

Direct gaze; real-anchor gaze; distance gaze; lowered eyes; profile; over-shoulder return; footstep gaze; brief closed eyes; candid reaction. Motion: parallel walk, approach, walk away/look back, side step, half turn, safe gentle spin, stop after two steps, react to breeze, sit/rise, or reach toward a real anchor.

For 16 photos use at least 10 hand configurations, 10 leg/foot configurations, 6 torso directions, 5 gazes, 4 crops, 4 motion states, and 4 scene zones.
